So, dumb politician = most shit given. I don't mean dumb as in stupid.

# Carl! Did you see the news?

Me neither. But! If you did?

You might have (hopefully have not) noticed that the news is full of people giving so much shit about all the stuff. The darker and more awful the shit is, the more time the shit gets on the news and in the media, and the ugly circle repeats and expands. It's a constant downward spiral. It would be neat if the news outlets and broadcasters could spin things in perhaps a slightly more hollistic way than "oh, shit". And the news also love to give a shit about other things giving a shit. So if other types of outlets take up content space, like say podcasts or social media, the news outlets get angsty and annoyed, and mope about needing more funding (from the government of course, or your wallet) so they can keep existing.

It's also hilarious (it is not) that content (I dislike that word, maybe I'll write about that sometime) creators, herein also news outlets, seem to think that people (that's you, and me) live to consume their content. Here's news for you who broadcast news; Most people do not live to consume, they live to feel, eat, drink, love, create, relax, to enjoy time with their family and friends doing brain work like reading real books and solving puzzles. Sit down and shut up. Please start writing deep and impactful news. Maybe let people know how they can help or do good. Stop writing hogwash and clickbait, and especially stop generating your content with articial intelligence. As if human intelligence isn't shit enough sometimes. You're your own doom, gloom, and downfall. I'd almost write good riddance, but I won't.

You go in circles, you even have seasons to fit your content into. You don't even cover all things that go on at large in the world. You zoom in and hyperfocus on a couple places in the world (I'm inclined to think you're regulated (lobbied) on who and where), you don't even cover a wide range of topics, it's mostly war, and weather. Two of the most boring topics ever, society has done more since we were cave people. Maybe the news didn't reach you yet.

# Politics

Back to start. Where was I..

Oh, yes. Politicians and giving a shit. Yeah, they give a shit about everything and nothing. Mostly nothing. What if they gave less of a shit about a whole bunch of things, like stuff they don't govern? Perhaps they'd get an act or two together and make an actual change in the oh so short amount of time (it's not short, it's four years, but they love to say it's short to get pity and a reelection to (not) try again) that they represent the people. That would be something. But oh no, suddenly they'd be out of work. Better keep giving a shit so they forget about why they grew up to be an important politician in the first place!

# Less shit?

Here is a challenge for you. Tomorrow, give one less shit about one thing in your life. And don't be a dick. Perhaps, yeah this is decent, give one less shit about some random criticism your work gets at work (yes, your work, it's your work that's criticized, not you, don't take it personal). Feel how you're less affected, and perhaps your day gets a tiny bit better!

# Oh, and

I'm not really saying don't give a shit. I'm definitely not saying that. What I mean by don't give a shit, is that of all the things you can do in this world, diving into problems you don't know how to or aren't willing to truly solve, shouldn't be given a shit about. What you then should give a shit about, is finding people who will give a shit about that, and let them do their thing, preferably without interference. So they can give a shit about the stuff you don't truly give a shit about. Hehe.

In other words, _don't give a shit about shit you don't (or want to) know, give a shit about shit you (or want to) know_.

There's a lot of good in letting go.
