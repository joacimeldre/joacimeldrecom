# Idea

I'm not entirely sure where the idea for Stack a Snack came from. It's a mix of everything that floats around in my head.

The idea took shape in my head before Falling Block Jam, and I didn't know Falling Block Jam was a thing until I had seriously started to go into make mode. So that was a happy coincident. A bit sad about not being able to officially join the jam, the jam has a zero generative artificial intelligence policy, and I have no idea how to make a game like this with my own hands. Fair enough. Although it feels wrong to even say that I participated in the Falling Block Jam, then? Since generated code isn't allowed.

I had fun anyway though! I like to draw pixels, and design games.

Anyway, I've always liked cake.

So that was it for the collectibles in the game.

I also like bunnies, who doesn't? So that was the player and enemy characters sorted. The big blue one is quite derpy, and I love that. I like wonky, uneven, different. I try to find that feeling in most of what I make. Also pleased with the simple animations, 2-3 sprites at most.

# Sprite (pixels)

I drew all the game sprites in 8x8 using [Pixelorama](https://pixelorama.org/), which is a really great pixel editing software. The label image is drawn as 128x128 and imported into the label section.

Gray bunny (player sprite) is made up of 4 sprites (head, body, tail, mouth). See image below for anatomy lesson.

![Gray bunny](../images/posts/graybunny.webp "Gray bunny exploded and assembled view")

Blue bunny is made up of 13 sprites where 1 is a slot and only show when chewing (right cheek, left on screen).

Cakes are made up of 3 sprites at most (3 layer cake stack), they're 6x2 pixels in size. There's a pink and a blue version, with the equal patterns.
